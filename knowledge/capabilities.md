# Camera review capabilities

NVIDIA's local real-time VLM describes activity on up to four approved RTSP
cameras. Each camera has its own preview and saved observations with capture
times, window snapshots and analysis timings. Up to five independent goals
apply to the configured cameras. Possible matches appear in Chat. Membrane
indexes captions and original images for
retrospective questions.
Core/SDK retain lifecycle and authenticated tool authority. Credentials stay in
the secure platform contract. Captions are fallible descriptions for human review.
